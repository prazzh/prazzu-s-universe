Prajwal — still becoming

I’m the kind of person who can stay quiet for hours and still have a thousand thoughts running through my head. I’m an introvert with a soft heart, but there’s always a lot going on behind the silence. I don’t need to be the loudest person in the room to have big dreams.

I have a soft heart. I care deeply, I notice little things, and I try to treat people with kindness. I’m still learning how to protect my peace without becoming a person I’m not.

Life hasn’t always been gentle with me. I’ve had a tough life, lost things I loved, and had days that made me question where I was going. But every loss has taught me something about resilience, patience, and starting again.

But I’m still here. Still dreaming. Still trying. Maybe that is the most honest thing about me: I keep going.

I don’t want to become someone cold because life was hard. I want to become someone who can look back at everything and say — I made it without losing my heart.

the things that keep me alive

Cars have always had a different kind of magic for me. The sound, the design, the engineering, the feeling of watching a machine built with purpose. It’s one of those things I can get lost in without even trying.

Then there’s Formula 1 — speed, pressure, precision, strategy, mistakes, comebacks. I love the intensity of it. Every tiny decision matters, and sometimes the difference between losing and winning is made in seconds.

Music is different. Music doesn’t need an explanation. Sometimes one song can say everything I couldn’t, and sometimes the right track can completely change a moment.

And travelling? I want to see more of this world. Different roads, different cities, different skies — places that remind me there is always something beyond the life I already know.
